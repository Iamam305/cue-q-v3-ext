## Detailed description

```
Cue Q keeps your team’s best AI prompts in one library—and the Chrome extension puts them where you actually chat.

Sign in with your Cue Q account, then browse, search, and insert prompts without hopping tabs to copy and paste.

What you can do
• Open the toolbar popup to search your library, create or edit prompts, and organize folders
• Share prompts with your team or keep them private
• On ChatGPT or Claude, press Alt+I to search and insert a prompt into the composer
• Copy prompt text when you would rather paste it yourself

The extension talks only to cue-q.com. It does not read your ChatGPT or Claude conversations. It does not inject ads or track your browsing.

Create a free library at https://cue-q.com, then sign in from the extension.

Need help? https://cue-q.com/support
```
